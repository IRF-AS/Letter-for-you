A little explanation from me to you.

Dear Ruhi, ♡

Maybe it's a little too late to explain all this, but mujhe bolne do.

As you know, Diwali ke baad se main kaafi kam online aa raha hoon. Maybe tumhe kabhi feel hota hoga ki main jaan-bujhkar aisa kar raha hoon, ya tumse baat karna avoid kar raha hoon. But nahi Ruhi, honestly, aisa bilkul nahi hai.

Actually, main kuch zyada hi faltu cheezon mein busy ho gaya hoon. It's like I've become lazy, something I never used to feel before. Jab mere exams the tab bhi idk meri body aisa feel karti thi jaise main 40 years old hoon or something. 😭

But that's not the actual reason.

The truth is, main chah kar bhi utna active nahi reh paa raha hoon. I know, sunne mein kaafi weird lagta hai. Main tumse baat karna chahta hoon, tumhare saath time spend karna chahta hoon, but idk kyun main khud ko hi ek ajeeb tarah se dekhne laga hoon.

Maybe I'm less socially interactive, maybe I overthink too much, or maybe I simply consider myself more useless than I actually am.

Aur isi wajah se kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki main uss level ka hi nahi hoon jiske saath tum deserve karti ho baat karna.

Because seriously, Ruhi, what have I given you?

Not even an edit of your Diwali outfit, not even those little gifts, chocolates, or creative surprises.

I feel bad because main tumhe itna chahta hoon ki mann karta hai tumhe roz chhoti-chhoti gifts doon, chocolates doon, decorated letters bana kar doon, aur tumhari aur meri ek cute si story create karun. Something that I can personally tumhare saamne laakar doon, aur tum use read karo.

And it's not that I can't do these things. Mujhe nahi aata toh seekh jaaunga. But the actual problem is privacy, my mental state, and this habit of waiting for a perfect time. And honestly, that perfect time never seems to arrive.

Maybe I need a little support from you too.

And haan Ruhi, kabhi bhi ye mat sochna ki I love you any less now. (LOL 😭)

Nahi Ruhi, you're someone I genuinely imagine in my future. You're my future wife, and I can't even imagine replacing the place you've made in my heart. That place is yours, permanently. ❤️

And I know you're also dealing with things in your own life, especially with your family and Dad. Dekho Ruhi, whenever you feel like sharing something, you can always tell me. You don't have to keep everything to yourself.

I can't guarantee that kal se main suddenly bahut zyada online aa jaaunga, but I'll genuinely try to improve.

And you know what? If you ever ask me how I want us to spend time together, I'd say:

Why don't we study together for just one hour? ♡

At least mujhe bhi feel hoga ki I've done something productive.

And why don't we give ourselves another 1–2 hours just for us? Main apne edits karunga, tum bhi apne editing comeback ki preparation karna. 😭❤️

That way, dono ko kuch productive karne ka satisfaction bhi milega, aur hum saath mein time bhi spend kar paayenge.

I don't want us to feel like spending time together means wasting time. I want us to grow individually while still being part of each other's lives.

So, think about it, okay?

Btw, ye letter likhte-likhte already 5:04 AM ho gaye hain. 😭 I don't even know when I'm finally going to send this to you, but hopefully you'll read every single word.

Chalo phir, subah milte hain.

I can't wait for your reply. I'm missing you, Ruhi.

Good night, ♡

Yours, always ❤️
